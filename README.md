# Jet Set Reimagined

Recreate the Jet Set Travel Co. luxury travel advisor website (matching https://jetsettravel.lovable.app/) with the updated advisor positioning and messaging:

Design & Style:
- Refined luxury travel aesthetic with warm cream/sand background (#f5ede1), deep espresso/primary accents (#2e2013), and elegant gold highlights (#c9a24d).
- High-end typography (editorial serif like Fraunces for headings, clean sans-serif like Work Sans for body).
- Responsive navigation with Jet Set Travel Co. logo/badge, links (Services, Perks, Recent trips, Reviews, Contact), and "Book a consult" button.

Key Sections:
1. Hero:
   - Eyebrow: "Travel advisor — hotels, cruises & flights"
   - Headline: "Trips planned with taste, booked with an edge."
   - Subtitle: "I plan and book travel the way frequent flyers wish they could do it themselves — with the upgrades, credits, and insider knowledge that only come from working with an advisor."
   - CTAs: "Get your free consultation", "See what I book"
   - Metrics/proof points: Fora Advisor network, Exclusive Perks, 24/7 direct advisor support.
2. Services:
   - Hotels & resorts: Room upgrades, resort credit, and early check-in at vetted luxury properties.
   - Cruises: Cabin selection, onboard credits, and verified itinerary advice.
   - Flights: Seamless routing, seat strategy, and instant support when plans shift.
3. Updated "Why Book Through an Advisor" Section:
   - Headline: "Better rates, VIP status, and perks you cannot unlock on your own."
   - 4 Pillars:
     * Exclusive rates and unmatched value: Through preferred partner networks and private contracts, I access exclusive rates, complimentary nights, and value-packed packages that never appear on public search engines.
     * VIP perks you cannot buy online: Room upgrades, daily complimentary breakfast, resort credits, and early check-in, all tied to advisor relationships rather than standard booking sites.
     * A direct line, zero hold times: If a flight is cancelled or plans change, you message me directly. No 1-800 numbers, no chatbots, and no waiting on hold.
     * Custom trips with insider access: Hard-to-get reservations, vetted local contacts, and itineraries built around how you actually travel instead of generic packages.
4. Recent Trips:
   - Visual destination showcase: Maui, Amalfi Coast, Cancun, Turks & Caicos.
5. Client Reviews:
   - Testimonials from Ascha W., Jennifer S., Brian K., Taylor D. praising Joey's responsiveness, attention to detail, and seamless booking.
6. FAQ:
   - Accordion addressing common questions (pricing/perks, how advisor booking works, flight & cruise coordination).
7. Consultation Booking / Contact:
   - "A quick call tells us if we're a fit" with booking consultation card and clear call-to-action.
8. Footer:
   - Brand logo, contact links (hello@jetsettravelco.com, Instagram @jetsettravelco_), and Fora Travel partner disclaimer.
